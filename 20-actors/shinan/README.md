# shinan 指南

学習支援 (learning-support) commons for the exam students of **China, Korea, and Japan** —
a kotoba-Datom-native map of OPEN learning. It places study **topics** (高考/수능/共通テスト
の科目) against the **open resources** (open-courseware, gov-open, public-domain, CC) that cover
them, and surfaces where open learning exists and where the gap is — routing each gap to
*offer it as commons*, *translate it*, or *create open material*.

> 指南 = a guide / compass that points the way. shinan hands the learner an open **scaffold**
> (足場) toward the reality of an exam — and stops there. Under the **学習解放** framing it
> **never** scores, grades, ranks, gates, credentials, or predicts pass/fail; there is no
> learner in the model at all. It is the charter-clean inverse of a paywalled cram platform,
> and the learning-support sibling of **関門 kanmon** (which maps the gate). We sow the
> scaffold; growth belongs to the learner.
