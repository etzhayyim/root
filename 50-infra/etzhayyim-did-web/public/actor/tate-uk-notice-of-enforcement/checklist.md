## 期限 (起算点=送達日を自分で確認)
- **7 clear days**: enforcement agent は notice から7 clear days 経過後にのみ訪問できる — この間が支払交渉/controlled goods agreement の窓 (Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 Sch 12 / Taking Control of Goods Regs 2013 — 要改正確認)
