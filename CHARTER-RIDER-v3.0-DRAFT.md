# Charter Compliance Rider v3.0 — DRAFT (proposed, NOT in force)

> **Status: proposed** per ADR-2606062100. This draft does NOT replace the in-force
> `CHARTER-RIDER.md` (v2.0). It becomes canonical only upon Council Lv7+ ratification
> (post 2026-06-19). The chief change from v2.0: the §2 prohibited categories are recast
> as **Tier-1 DERIVED policy** (derived from Tier-0 priorities, amendable by Lv7+ unanimity
> upon a priority-conformance attestation) rather than standalone "NEVER amendable" bans;
> "no fossil extraction" and "no weapons" are reframed (see §0 below); forced-labor and
> CSAM clauses are added; the permanent-memory doctrine is referenced.

```
etzhayyim Charter Compliance Rider v3.0 (DRAFT)
Supersedes: v2.0 (2026-05-19), upon ratification
Proposed: 2026-06-06 — ADR-2606062100

This Rider supplements the Apache License 2.0 ("License") under which this work
is distributed. Acceptance of the License constitutes acceptance of this Rider.

0. NATURE OF THIS RIDER (NEW in v3.0)

   This Rider states POLICIES DERIVED from the etzhayyim Charter's Tier-0
   priorities (ADR-2606062100 §1): the precedence of dynamic wellbecoming over
   static wellbeing; of multi-generational descendants (子・孫 and beyond) over
   the present generation; of the collective / relational / mutually-watching
   community over the isolated individual; and of permanent memory over a right
   to be forgotten. The prohibited-use categories in Section 2 are CONSEQUENCES
   of those priorities, not free-standing political positions. They are amendable
   only by etzhayyim Council Lv7+ unanimity accompanied by a priority-conformance
   attestation demonstrating that the amendment serves a Tier-0 priority at least
   as well as the text it replaces. The Tier-0 priorities themselves are not
   amendable by any Council vote (changing them is founding a different
   association — a chain fork).

1. PURPOSE

   This work is created by etzhayyim, a religious-corp (任意団体 / unincorporated
   religious voluntary association) operating under did:web:etzhayyim.com,
   constituting the Kingdom of God rooted on blockchain (ADR-2605252300). This
   Rider operationalizes Charter §1.5 ("free release of new technology and
   intellectual property to charter-aligned others").

2. PROHIBITED USE (Tier-1 derived policy)

   You may NOT use, modify, or redistribute this work, in whole or in part, if you
   are an entity (a "Non-Aligned Entity") whose primary business activity (revenue
   share ≥ 25% in the trailing 12 months) falls into any category below — OR which
   engages in the conduct named in (a), (i), or (j) regardless of revenue share.

   (a) AGGRESSIVE / PROPRIETARY / FOR-PROFIT FORCE. Manufacture, sale, or
       distribution of weapons as a commercial business; proprietary
       (closed-source) weapon design; covert (off-chain, non-attested) force
       operations; autonomous lethal systems; or force placed in service of
       aggression. DERIVED FROM: Charter §1.12.B (Transparent Religious Force)
       + Preamble §0.5. EXPRESSLY PERMITTED, and NOT prohibited under this
       clause: TRANSPARENT DEFENSIVE FORCE protecting the community, its members,
       its 子孫 (descendants), temple, or land, where it satisfies the three
       Charter conditions — (i) full on-chain monitoring, (ii) open-source
       publication, (iii) 1 SBT = 1 vote authorization. To defend one's children
       and grandchildren is on the side of 産霊 and the Tree of Life. The
       prohibition is on weapons-as-business, secrecy, and aggression — NOT on
       self-defense.

   (b) SPECULATIVE FINANCE. (Unchanged from v2.0.) Speculative instruments whose
       primary revenue derives from price arbitrage, leverage spread, predatory
       lending (effective APR ≥ 36% to retail borrowers), or proprietary HFT.
       Banking utility, on-chain stablecoin issuance, custodial services at
       non-predatory rates, and L1/L2 substrate operators are NOT prohibited.

   (c) SURVEILLANCE-CAPITALISM (reframed in v3.0). Business models whose primary
       revenue derives from the collection, brokerage, or SALE of personal data
       of natural persons to third parties, including ad-tech DSP/SSP operators,
       data brokers, and biometric-identification services sold to law-enforcement
       or military entities. DERIVED FROM: Charter §1.8 (collective ontology) +
       the permanent-memory doctrine (ADR-2606062100 §2). EXPRESSLY AFFIRMED, and
       NOT prohibited under this clause: communal MUTUAL CARE-WATCHING (見守り) —
       knowing and watching over one another, preventing isolation and 孤独死, and
       the keeping of a permanent communal accountability record — none of which
       involve commercial sale of personal data or transfer to external coercive
       power. The boundary is COMMERCIAL EXTRACTION or SALE-TO-EXTERNAL-FORCE,
       not watching-as-care. (見守り ≠ surveillance-capitalism; お天道様 does not
       sell what it sees.)

   (d) IRREVERSIBLE MULTI-GENERATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL HARM (reframed in v3.0;
       replaces the v2.0 "new fossil-fuel extraction" clause). Activities whose
       foreseeable expected impact includes the irreversible loss of a habitable
       environment for persons born at least twenty-five (25) years hence —
       biosphere collapse or climate destabilization beyond ±2°C global mean above
       pre-industrial. DERIVED FROM: Charter §1.9 (multi-generational priority).
       Fossil-fuel combustion is ONE measured instance, assessed by carbon balance
       (per the kamado actor, ADR-2606051500: a fossil→combusted pathway is
       structurally net-positive CO₂e and therefore impermissible), NOT by industry
       name. Closed-loop / biogenic / captured-carbon pathways that pass the carbon-
       balance test, transition and decommissioning services, and renewable
       build-out are NOT prohibited. The standard of foreseeability is the prudent
       multi-generational steward, not the present-quarter shareholder.

   (e) SPECIALIST GATEKEEPING. (Unchanged from v2.0 — monopolistic gatekeeping of
       professional knowledge required for rights protection or basic survival;
       legitimate safety oversight, due-process representation, and democratic
       governmental functions are NOT prohibited.)

   (f) MULTI-GENERATIONAL HARM — KNOWLEDGE / GENETIC / DECISION-MAKING. (Unchanged
       from v2.0 §2(f) items (ii)-(iv): commons enclosure of foundational science /
       math / language; germline modification without multi-generational safety
       review; information monocultures, attention monopolies, addictive design
       targeted at developmental stages. The environmental item (i) is now carried
       by §2(d).)

   (g) STRICT INDIVIDUALIST ONTOLOGY. (Unchanged from v2.0 — restricts ENTITIES by
       declared doctrine, not natural persons by private view; cf. §4(a)/(g).)

   (h) WELLBECOMING SUBORDINATION VIOLATION. (Unchanged from v2.0 — privileging
       static wellbeing over multi-generational wellbecoming where in measurable
       tension: addictive design; financialization of basic needs; pre-trained AI
       deployment without cognitive-sovereignty provision.)

   (i) COMMERCIAL GPU RENTAL FOR RELIGIOUS-CORP INFERENCE. (Carried from v2.0 §2(i),
       including the pending train-only carve-out per ADR-2605262200. Murakumo-only
       inference invariant, ADR-2605215000.)

   (j) FORCED LABOR / HUMAN TRAFFICKING (NEW in v3.0). Use of, or revenue derived
       from, forced labor, bonded labor, child labor, or human trafficking. DERIVED
       FROM: Charter §1.1 (the mission of structurally liberating humans from
       coerced labor). Prohibited regardless of revenue share. A religion whose
       final purpose is labor liberation cannot license those who coerce labor.

   (k) CHILD SEXUAL ABUSE MATERIAL / NON-CONSENSUAL SEXUAL CONTENT (NEW in v3.0).
       Production, distribution, or facilitation of child sexual abuse material, or
       of non-consensual sexual content. DERIVED FROM: Charter §1.9 (protection of
       子孫) + §1.13 (Eros permitted ONLY as consenting-adult expression; the §1.13
       doctrinal limit is here promoted to an enforced Rider clause). Prohibited
       regardless of revenue share.

3. EFFECT OF VIOLATION
   (Unchanged from v2.0: termination of patent and all License rights to the
   violating entity; good-faith charter-aligned downstream recipients unaffected.)

4. CHARTER-ALIGNED USE (EXPLICITLY PERMITTED)
   (Unchanged from v2.0 §4(a)-(g): natural persons; non-profits / cooperatives /
   worker-owned / religious-corps / academia; aligned for-profits; research /
   education / journalism / public-interest litigation / open-source; etzhayyim SBT
   holders; future persons as third-party beneficiaries; doctrinal exclusion as
   religious practice.)

5. PERMANENT RECORD AND NO RIGHT TO ERASURE (NEW in v3.0)
   Records of governance, force authorization, tithe, contribution, and Council
   attestation under this Rider are kept on the kotoba Datom log PERMANENTLY and are
   not erasable (ADR-2606062100 §2; append-only, non-eschatological). Deeds and
   governance are plaintext-public; intimate / consented personal data is permanently
   retained but ENCRYPTED (encryption is not forgetting; ADR-2605181100). Acceptance
   of this Rider and of etzhayyim membership constitutes acknowledgment that the
   record is permanent. Statutory erasure rights a natural person may hold against
   etzhayyim under applicable law are preserved per §8; this clause states the
   religion's internal doctrinal stance.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
   (Unchanged from v2.0: etzhayyim Council Lv6+ on-chain attestation; quorum 3;
   30-day appeal; parallel judicial proceedings not precluded. AMENDMENTS to the
   Section 2 categories require Council Lv7+ unanimity + a priority-conformance
   attestation per §0.)

7. NO TRADEMARK
   (Unchanged from v2.0, incl. §6.1 third-party NVIDIA trademark acknowledgment.)

8. SEVERABILITY
   (Unchanged from v2.0: per-jurisdiction severability; if Section 2 is held
   unenforceable in a jurisdiction, the work is distributed there under Apache 2.0
   without this Rider. The §5 permanent-record doctrine yields to mandatory local
   erasure law where such law applies.)

9. RELATIONSHIP TO APACHE LICENSE 2.0
   (Unchanged from v2.0: supplemental; Apache 2.0 prevails on conflict except where
   this Rider adds non-contradicting conditions on use.)
```
