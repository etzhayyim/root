# Primary Sources By Category (news.etzhayyim.com)

This file seeds official primary-source channels per category track (C1-C11).

Principles
- Prefer issuer/regulator/association originals over summaries.
- For numbers (market size, shipments, rankings), use the underlying dataset or issuer IR.
- Keep the raw source URL in article metadata when available.

## C1 Automotive (自動車)
- OEM / supplier IR and press releases (product launches, recalls, production plans, earnings).
- Japan regulators and statistics: MLIT, METI (regulatory notices, policy, production/trade stats).
- Industry associations and technical bodies: JAMA, JSAE.
- Safety and standards bodies: UNECE (WP.29) where applicable; Japanese standards notices.

## C2 Semiconductor Equipment/Materials (半導体製造装置・材料)
- Equipment/material vendor IR and product announcements (lithography, etch, deposition, metrology, resist, wafers).
- Japan policy/statistics: METI (semiconductor strategy, export controls, industrial policy updates).
- Industry associations: SEAJ (Semiconductor Equipment Association of Japan), JEITA, SEMI (Japan/global).
- Standards/spec bodies: SEMI standards, JEDEC (packaging/memory where relevant).

## C3 Robotics/FA (ロボティクス/FA)
- Vendor IR and product releases (industrial robots, cobots, servo drives, controllers).
- Industry associations: Japan Robot Association (JARA), IFR (global shipment/installation baselines).
- Factory automation standards and reference docs: PLC vendor manuals, industrial networking specs.

## C4 Anime/IP (アニメ/IP)
- Rights-holder originals: publisher/studio/production committee announcements.
- Distribution originals: broadcaster/streaming platform press releases and program pages.
- Licensing originals: official license announcements from licensors/licensees.
- Industry associations: Association of Japanese Animations (AJA) for industry reports.

## C5 Medical Devices (医療機器)
- Japan regulators: PMDA and MHLW (approvals, safety alerts, device classifications).
- Global regulators when the device is ex-Japan: FDA, EU MDR public notices.
- Clinical trial registries for claims about studies: jRCT, ClinicalTrials.gov.
- Manufacturer originals: IFU (instructions for use), safety notices, field actions.

## C6 Precision Optics/Camera (精密機器: カメラ/光学)
- Manufacturer originals: product pages, press releases, firmware/update notices.
- Industry associations: CIPA (shipment statistics, standards references).
- Standards/spec sources: lens mount/format specs from official issuers when public.

## C7 Robotics + Industrial Automation (産業オートメーション)
- Automation vendor originals: PLC/SCADA/industrial IoT product notes and security advisories.
- Industry associations: JARA, IFR; relevant automation consortia/spec issuers.
- Factory retrofit and safety: regulator notices and safety standard updates.

## C8 Machine Tools + FA (工作機械・FA)
- Builder originals: machine announcements, options, controller updates.
- Industry associations: JMTBA (Japan Machine Tool Builders' Association), JIMTOF official releases.
- Standards: JIS/ISO updates relevant to machining, metrology, and safety.

## C9 Music/VTuber/Live Entertainment (音楽・VTuber・ライブ)
- Agency/label originals: artist releases, tours, contracts, earnings.
- Platform originals: YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music, ticketing platforms (policy/product updates).
- Rights bodies: JASRAC for licensing/royalties references.
- Event organizer originals: venue/promoter announcements and official schedules.

## C10 Fashion/Textile Materials (ファッション・繊維素材)
- Brand/manufacturer originals: product launches, sustainability disclosures, earnings.
- Japan policy/statistics: METI (industry policy and trade where relevant).
- Standards and certification issuers: ISO updates; certification program issuers when cited.
- Trade fair organizers: official exhibitor/news releases for major events.

## C11 Tourism/Inbound (観光・インバウンド)
- Japan Tourism Agency (観光庁) and JNTO (visitor stats, releases, policy).
- Transport operators: airlines/railways official timetables and press releases.
- Local government/DMO originals: campaigns, events, and official statistics.
- Border/visa policy: official government announcements when travel rules change.
